Privacy policy

For the eRideSafe iOS app · Last updated November 7, 2026

eRideSafe provides e-bike classification, safety education, local riding maps, community safety reports, and Ride Mode. This policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the app.

Information we collect

eRideSafe handles the following information:

  • Account identifier: Firebase Authentication automatically creates a guest account with a unique identifier. You do not need to provide a name, email address, or password. This identifier connects your saved profile and reports to the same account.
  • Classification and learning progress: Your selected bike class, setup status, completed lessons, certificate date, and related timestamps are saved on your device and uploaded to your cloud profile. Individual classification quiz answers and assessment answers are not uploaded by the current app.
  • Safety reports: Submitted reports include the category, description, location coordinates, submission time, selected bike class, and account identifier.
  • Optional photos: If you select a photo for a report, the app compresses and uploads that image. The app uses Apple’s photo picker and does not scan your entire photo library.
  • Technical information: Firebase processes information such as IP addresses, device and software information, and diagnostic data to operate, protect, and improve its services.
  • Support communications: If you email us, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to provide.

Location and motion

With your permission, eRideSafe uses your location to display nearby riding guidance, calculate GPS speed, provide Ride Mode alerts, and attach coordinates to reports. The app requests location access while in use and does not configure continuous background location tracking.

Submitting a safety report currently requires an available location. Its coordinates are uploaded and shared with other app users. Location may be precise or approximate depending on your device settings.

Ride Mode processes speed and device motion for ride information and alerts. The current app does not upload continuous ride routes, speed histories, or raw motion measurements to its database. Alert preferences are stored locally.

How we use information

We use information to operate guest accounts, save classification and course progress, tailor riding guidance, display community reports, provide app functionality, respond to support requests, and maintain the service.

The current app does not display advertisements or use your information for advertising tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

Information shared with others

Community safety reports are shared with other eRideSafe users. Report descriptions, coordinates, categories, submission dates, bike classifications, and attached photos may be viewed by those users. Shared database records also contain the submitting account’s identifier, although the app does not display it as a public profile name.

Other users can export report details. Avoid including names, contact details, identifying images, private addresses, or other information you do not want shared.

Google Firebase provides authentication and cloud storage services. Learn more about its handling of information in Firebase’s privacy information. Apple provides mapping and other system services under its applicable privacy practices.

When you use the system share sheet, the selected information is shared with the recipients or apps you choose. Sharing a ride location sends a location link; it does not start continuous live location sharing through eRideSafe.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to address misuse and protect the service or others.

Storage and retention

App settings and cached information are stored on your device. Account profiles, submitted reports, and uploaded photos are stored in Firebase. Service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

The current app does not automatically expire account profiles, reports, or photos after a fixed period. Records remain until deleted or otherwise removed. Service-provider logs and backups follow their applicable retention processes. Copies exported by other users may remain outside our control.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can change location permissions through iOS Settings. Disabling location limits location-dependent features and report submission. Attaching photos is optional.

You can delete a report belonging to your current account by opening its details and selecting the trash button. If a report or photo remains accessible afterward, contact us.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your account and associated information, email annirudh.eridesafe@gmail.com. We may request information needed to identify your account and verify your request. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens.

Deleting the app does not automatically delete cloud information. Restarting the classification setup does not delete course progress or reports.

Children’s information

The current app does not collect age information or provide parental-consent controls. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal information can contact annirudh.eridesafe@gmail.com to request assistance or deletion.

Security

eRideSafe uses authentication and access controls designed to protect private account information. Community reports are intentionally shared with other authenticated users. No method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee complete security.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or its practices change. The latest version will display an updated revision date. For privacy questions or requests, contact annirudh.eridesafe@gmail.com.
