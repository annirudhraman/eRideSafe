Classification quiz and learning progress

To update your classification, open Settings in eRideSafe and choose Retake device class quiz. Restarting the classification setup keeps your course progress and reports.

The course certificate recognizes completion of the app’s lessons and assessment. It is not a government-issued riding license.

Maps and Ride Mode

The local riding map covers Sammamish, Washington. Allow location access for eRideSafe in iOS Settings to use location-dependent features. Ride Mode needs an available GPS signal. If the app cannot match your location to a road or trail, check your Precise Location setting.

Follow posted signs and current local rules. Stop in a safe place before interacting with the app.

Reports and photos

Submitted reports and photos are shared with other app users. To delete your own report, open its details and tap the trash button.

For help with an inappropriate report, a photo that remains visible, or another reporting issue, email support with enough detail to identify the content. Avoid including unnecessary personal information about other people.

Privacy and data requests

To request access, correction, or deletion of your account and associated data, email annirudh.eridesafe@gmail.com. We may need additional information to identify your guest account and verify the request.

Deleting the app does not automatically delete cloud records. Read our privacy policy for details about information handling and your choices.

Emergencies

eRideSafe is not an emergency reporting service. Contact the appropriate emergency service directly if you need urgent assistance.
